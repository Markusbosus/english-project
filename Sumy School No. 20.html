About us

Welcome to our school

Communal Institution “Sumy General Secondary School of I–III Levels No. 20” in Sumy, Sumy Region, welcomes students from primary school to graduation. We follow the New Ukrainian School concept and build a safe, open and caring environment where pupils, teachers and parents work together.

📚

New Ukrainian School

Modern, competence-based education that develops curiosity and critical thinking.

🛡️

Safe & caring

Anti-bullying policy, a school psychologist and a medical service support every child.

💻

Distance learning

Flexible online learning ensures education continues in any circumstances.
